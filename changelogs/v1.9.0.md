### ✨ New Features

- Added a status bar that shows app version, update status and more to the bottom of the app
- Move, resize, or dock notes and resources; their layout and unfinished edits are kept

### 🛡️ Improvements

- Refreshed forms and pop-ups with clearer layouts
- Made game and character card sizes consistent
- Updated the header, startup overlay, and app icon with better images
- Improved video backups with progress, cancellation, and warnings for oversized exports
- Added startup progress and retry options; reopening the app brings the existing window forward
- Backups now include selected games and characters even when they have no combos

### 🛠️ Fixes

- Cover art searches no longer fail for certain game titles
- Removed brief flashes of empty pages when navigating to a new page
- Fixed malformed circular motion (`spd`, `360`, `720`, `1080`) joystick icons
- Repeated saves no longer create duplicate entries
- Custom accent colors keep buttons readable, and notifications follow theme changes
