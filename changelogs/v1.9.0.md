### ✨ New Features

- Reworked notes and resources into a movable, resizable notebook that can dock on either side and remembers its layout.
- Added app version and update status to the bottom bar, including download progress and offline update details.

### 🛡️ Improvements

- Refreshed app windows with clearer layouts, better scrolling, and support for smaller screens. The Notation Guide adds clickable examples and a preview that stays visible.
- Improved video backups with progress, cancellation, and warnings for oversized exports.
- Made game and character card sizes consistent and improved combo layouts in narrow windows.
- Updated the header and startup logos, with sharper app icons.
- Added startup progress and retry options; reopening the app brings the existing window forward.

### 🛠️ Fixes

- Fixed cover searches failing for certain game titles.
- Removed brief flashes of empty pages when browsing games and characters.
- Improved motion icons and fixed circular inputs (`spd`, `360`, `720`, `1080`) across all notation styles.
